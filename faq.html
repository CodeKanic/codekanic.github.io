FAQ

Can I use these in a game I sell?
Yes. See the License page.
Which engine versions do they work with?
Each listing says the version it was tested on.
Do I get updates?
Yes, free, through itch.io where you bought it.
Something's broken. What now?
Open an issue at github.com/CodeKanic/support. I'll fix it and note it in the changelog.
How does buying work?
Templates are sold on itch.io. Clicking Buy opens itch checkout. itch handles payment and emails you a download link, and you can re-download any time from your itch library. I never see your card details.
Refunds?
If a template doesn't import or run as described, open an issue on the support tracker within 14 days of purchase. I'll refund it or fix it.
Are any free?
Yes, starter projects are on GitHub.
