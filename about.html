About

I'm Dawson Taylor, a self-taught game dev. I've been making games on my own for a while; you can play Spin Shot on my itch page.

Every project started with the same few days of setup, so I started turning that setup into templates. Codekanic is where they live.

I'd rather put out a few templates that work well than a pile that doesn't.
