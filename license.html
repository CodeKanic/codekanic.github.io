License

The short version:

  • You can use templates in personal and commercial projects, as many as you want.
  • You can change the code however you want.
  • You can't resell or share the template itself, changed or not, as a template, asset pack, or source bundle.
  • Free GitHub repos use the license listed in each repo (usually MIT).

Full terms

When you buy a Codekanic template, you get a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, modify, and ship the code and included assets in your own projects, including games and apps you sell. There is no royalty and no per-project fee.
